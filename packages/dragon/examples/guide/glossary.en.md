# Terms

The terms a guide page shows on cards and in its Terms tab.

### Order

The items a user picks and the address to send them to.

> Three apples to a house in Tokyo

### Order number

The number the shop gives an order it takes. The user checks the order with it.

> Order 1024

### Payment

Taking the money for an order. The goods are not shipped until it is done.

> Paying 12 dollars by card
